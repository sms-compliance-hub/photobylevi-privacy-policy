## SMS / Text Messaging Privacy Disclosure

Mobile opt-in, SMS consent, and phone numbers collected for SMS
communication purposes will **not** be shared with third parties or
affiliates for marketing purposes.

By opting in to receive SMS messages from **Photography By Levi**, you
agree to receive conversational messages related to photography appointments,
event coordination, session reminders, order updates, and other
service-related communications.

- **Message frequency:** Message frequency may vary. On average, 1–2
  messages per month.
- **Opt out:** Reply **STOP** to cancel at any time.
- **Help:** Reply **HELP** for assistance, or visit https://photobylevi.com
- **Message and data rates:** Message and data rates may apply.

**Verbal Opt-In:**
Customers may provide SMS consent verbally during a phone call or
in-person consultation. At the time of verbal consent, staff will inform
the customer of message frequency, applicable fees, opt-out instructions
(STOP), and how to get help (HELP). Consent is documented internally and
is not required as a condition of purchasing services.
